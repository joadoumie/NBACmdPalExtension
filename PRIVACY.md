# Privacy Policy — NBA Command Palette Extension

_Last updated: 2026-04-18_

This extension is a third-party, open-source tool. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the National Basketball Association (NBA), ESPN, or any NBA team**.

## What data the extension collects

**None.** The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information, telemetry, usage analytics, or crash reports.

- No account, sign-in, or identifier is required.
- No data is written to any server under the author's control.
- No cookies or trackers are used.

## What data the extension sends to third parties

The extension issues anonymous, unauthenticated HTTPS requests to ESPN's public scoreboard, team, and roster APIs (hosts under `*.espn.com` and `*.espncdn.com`) to retrieve league schedules, scores, rosters, standings, and statistics. These requests contain only the NBA data being queried (e.g., a team abbreviation or a date range). No user identity is included.

ESPN's own handling of those requests is governed by the [ESPN Privacy Policy](https://disneyprivacycenter.com/notice-to-end-users/).

## Children's privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. No sign-in is offered and no data leaves the user's device except the ESPN API calls described above.
